About MeshBench

What this independent screen-measuring and repair publication does—and does not claim.

Our focus

MeshBench is an independent educational website for people maintaining ordinary insect screens in windows and doors. We focus narrowly on practical measurement, compatible materials, common frame failures, serviceable door hardware and safe inspection procedures. Our purpose is to help a reader arrive at the correct next question before buying fabric, spline or parts.

How articles are prepared

The guides use publicly available manufacturer directions and safety guidance, including Phifer, Andersen, CDC and CPSC. We write original examples and organize repair decisions into inspect → act → verify. We do not imply manufacturers approved our interpretation, and we do not publish fabricated client stories, team credentials or hands-on tests.

What we cannot evaluate

No web form can physically inspect a frame, verify a product’s hidden groove geometry or approve a high-window installation. We cannot certify a child-safety guard, security screen or professional trade repair. When a product is nonstandard, consult its maker; when access or lifting is unsafe, use qualified help.

Corrections

If a specification is unclear or a reference has changed, contact the editorial address at chinachen05@gmail.com. Describe the page and disputed detail so the correction can be evaluated. Content prepared on 15 November 2026.
