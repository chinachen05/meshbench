Field note 21 / Hardware

Understand screen pull tabs and spring clips

Identify the hardware that holds a removable screen in place.

Editorially prepared 15 November 2026

The decision you need to make

Pull tabs help remove a screen; spring clips, plungers and latch hardware position or retain the frame in its window opening. They are not interchangeable components. A panel may be correctly sized yet fall loose if one of its retention parts is missing.

What to inspect first

  • Photograph the hardware while the screen is installed. The same tab can appear to be a latch until you see how the frame is supported.
  • Check for missing tension springs, cracked plastic pulls and seized plungers.
  • Compare the location and direction of every attachment piece with the original manufacturer configuration.

A practical sequence

  1. Confirm the exact product or frame profile before ordering repair hardware.
  2. Replace one damaged component at a time if safe to do so and recheck how the frame seats.
  3. Install hardware in the sequence specified for that frame, sometimes before new mesh goes in.
  4. After refitting, check that the panel is retained under normal window opening and closing.

Worked situation

A new screen fabric is tight but the panel rattles near its upper edge. A missing spring clip may be the reason; thicker mesh or spline will not solve it.

Common wrong turn

Pull tabs are not handles for supporting body weight, and screens are not child-fall barriers.

When to stop:

Do not remove a high external screen without safe indoor access. Contact the window manufacturer when replacement hardware is unfamiliar.
