Field note 18 / Repairs

Fix a screen that pulls out of its spline groove

Check groove condition, fabric thickness and spline size together.

Editorially prepared 15 November 2026

The decision you need to make

When mesh pulls free, the immediate temptation is to force in a thicker cord. That can damage the frame if the groove is narrow or the mesh is already thick. A correct repair starts by checking whether the cord, channel and fabric are still compatible.

What to inspect first

  • Look for looseness along one straight side versus several corners.
  • Inspect the spline for flattening, cracking or stretching from previous repairs.
  • Check the groove edge for dents or breaks that prevent a uniform grip.

A practical sequence

  1. Remove the failed section of spline and examine the mesh edge for tearing.
  2. Measure the channel and identify the fabric type; refer to the screen manufacturer’s sizing guidance.
  3. Test a small compatible sample in a sound section of the groove before committing to the full perimeter.
  4. Confirm the repaired mesh stays seated through gentle normal operation without pulling on it as a safety barrier.

Worked situation

A screen releases only beside a damaged corner key, while the remaining sides hold. Installing heavier spline throughout would miss the actual corner movement.

Common wrong turn

Do not mix leftover lengths of random diameter around the same frame; transitions in cord size produce inconsistent retention.

When to stop:

If the groove wall is torn or a corner of the panel separates, a new frame or professional repair may be safer and more reliable.
