Field note 28 / Care & safety

Store removable screens between seasons

Preserve frame shape, tabs and material labels during storage.

Editorially prepared 15 November 2026

The decision you need to make

Removable screens can be bent or crushed in storage even if they looked perfect when taken down. The biggest risks are stacked heavy objects, hooks that snag the fabric, and losing the small springs or tabs needed for reinstallation.

What to inspect first

  • Inspect each screen before storage so existing damage is not forgotten.
  • Record which window it belongs to and the orientation of any unusual corner hardware.
  • Choose a dry location where large frames can lie flat or stand supported without twisting.

A practical sequence

  1. Clean and fully dry screens according to their maker’s guidance.
  2. Label frames by room, window and orientation using removable tags rather than permanent markings on mesh.
  3. Protect fabric from sharp tools and do not stack heavy items on top.
  4. At reinstall time, verify that clips remain attached and the panel seats without force.

Worked situation

A pair of nearly identical bedroom screens differs only in spring-clip location. A small B1/B2 label avoids forcing the wrong one into a tight track next spring.

Common wrong turn

Leaning a tall frame diagonally against a narrow shelf for months can introduce a permanent bow.

When to stop:

If the windows require screens to be kept in place for building ventilation or legal requirements, check those requirements before seasonal removal.
