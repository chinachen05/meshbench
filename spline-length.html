Tool 03 / measured, not guessed

Spline Length Bench

Estimate round spline cord quantity from the actual groove path; do not infer diameter from length.

Calculate the perimeter of your spline groove.

How to interpret this result

For a standard rectangle the perimeter is 2 × (groove width + groove height). We multiply by the panel count and your reserve percentage. Spline diameter depends on groove profile and fabric; use the manufacturer’s compatibility chart, especially for thick pet or solar mesh.

Not an installation guarantee.

Confirm physical measurements on the actual frame and follow the manufacturer’s fabric and spline compatibility instructions. Do not use an insect screen as a child-safety or security barrier.

Read before cutting

For the difference between outside frame, screened opening and spline groove measurements, read Frame size vs mesh opening. For larger projects, record each actual panel in the Panel Notebook before purchasing materials.
