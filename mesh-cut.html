Tool 01 / measured, not guessed

Mesh Cut Planner

Calculate an initial piece of screen fabric from your screened opening and a user-defined working margin.

Enter your dimensions and calculate.

How to interpret this result

The calculation adds two selected margins to each opening dimension. It compares both orientations against the stated roll width. The chosen margin is a working allowance for holding and trimming the fabric, not a reduction or adjustment to the finished outside frame.

Not an installation guarantee.

Confirm physical measurements on the actual frame and follow the manufacturer’s fabric and spline compatibility instructions. Do not use an insect screen as a child-safety or security barrier.

Read before cutting

For the difference between outside frame, screened opening and spline groove measurements, read Frame size vs mesh opening. For larger projects, record each actual panel in the Panel Notebook before purchasing materials.
