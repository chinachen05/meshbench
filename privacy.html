Privacy policy

How this static website and its third-party advertising script may process information.

Local screen measurement data

The Panel Notebook stores records in localStorage on your own browser. MeshBench does not run an account system or transmit these records to a MeshBench database. You can delete individual records in the tool or clear site data in your browser. Export CSV if you need your own copy.

Advertising and third parties

This website includes the Google AdSense publisher verification script on editorial pages. Google and its partners may process browser/device information and use cookies or similar technologies if advertising is enabled. Refer to Google advertising technologies and Google privacy policy for their practices. Advertising behavior is governed by the publisher’s active AdSense settings and applicable law.

Consent obligations

Where regulations require advertising consent or a Google-certified consent management platform, the publisher must configure a compliant solution before serving personalized advertising. This static starter does not pretend to have collected consent.

Questions or privacy requests may be sent to chinachen05@gmail.com. We do not ask for account passwords or payment information.
