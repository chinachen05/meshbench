Editorial standards

Our rules for practical examples, technical claims, sources and corrections.

Problem-first, not volume-first

Every field note must answer a distinct practical question. We avoid broad generalities when a measurable check or clear decision is available. A short accurate instruction is better than filler designed to achieve an arbitrary word count.

Sources and scope

We prefer original manufacturer installation documents for spline and mesh claims, and public authorities for mosquito prevention and window safety. Every article links to relevant sources directly. Those links are citations, not sponsorship or endorsements.

Examples and calculators

Worked situations are illustrative and clearly presented as hypothetical; no example is claimed to be a verified customer project. Calculators display the selected input assumptions and do not automatically choose proprietary frame tolerances, spline diameter, security ratings or safety-critical installation methods.

Maintenance and correction process

Content was assembled and source-checked on 15 November 2026. When an error is reported, we check the original documentation and update the affected page as needed; dateModified should only change when a substantive page revision is made. Corrections: chinachen05@gmail.com.
